Trusted Across Diverse Sectors

From high-pressure oil rigs to pharmaceutical-grade piping, Siddhgiri Overseas supplies certified piping components that meet the exact demands of each industry we serve — across 40+ countries worldwide.

Oil & Gas

The oil and gas industry demands piping components that can withstand extreme pressures, corrosive environments, and temperature fluctuations. We supply ASTM/ASME-certified pipes, fittings, and flanges used in upstream, midstream, and downstream operations globally.

Seamless Pipes High-Pressure Flanges Buttweld Fittings Alloy Steel Grades NACE MR0175 Compliant

Petrochemical

Petrochemical plants handle highly reactive and corrosive fluids at elevated temperatures. Our stainless steel and nickel alloy piping systems deliver exceptional resistance to chemical attack, ensuring safe and uninterrupted operations in refineries and processing units.

Stainless Steel Pipes Nickel Alloy Fittings Duplex & Super Duplex ASTM A312 Grades Corrosion-Resistant

Power Plants

Power generation facilities — thermal, nuclear, and hydro — require piping that performs reliably under continuous high-temperature and high-pressure conditions. We supply boiler-grade pipes, alloy steel fittings, and flanges specifically designed for power plant applications.

Boiler Grade Pipes Alloy Steel P11/P22 High-Temp Flanges ASME B31.1 Creep-Resistant Grades

Shipbuilding & Marine

Marine environments are among the harshest for metal components — with constant exposure to saltwater, humidity, and mechanical stress. Our marine-grade stainless steel and copper-nickel piping products are widely used in shipbuilding yards and offshore platforms.

Marine Grade SS Copper-Nickel Pipes Offshore Flanges DNV / ABS Certified Salt-Water Resistant

Heavy Industry & Manufacturing

Steel plants, cement units, and heavy manufacturing facilities rely on durable carbon steel and alloy piping for fluid transfer, structural support, and utility systems. We supply bulk quantities with short lead times to keep production lines running.

Carbon Steel Pipes ERW & Seamless Forged Fittings Heavy-Wall Thickness Bulk Supply Available

Water Treatment

Water and wastewater treatment plants require piping that resists biological corrosion, scaling, and chemical treatment agents. Our stainless steel pipes and fittings are widely used in desalination plants, sewage treatment, and water distribution networks.

SS 304 / 316 Pipes Duplex Grades Chloride-Resistant Desalination Grade Sanitary Fittings

Food & Beverage

Hygienic piping is critical in food processing and beverage production. We supply food-grade stainless steel pipes with smooth interior finishes (Ra ≤ 0.8μm) that meet FDA and 3-A Sanitary Standards, ensuring zero contamination in production lines.

Food-Grade SS 316L Electropolished Finish Sanitary Fittings FDA Compliant 3-A Standard

Pharmaceuticals

Pharmaceutical manufacturing demands the highest standards of purity. Our ultra-clean, electropolished stainless steel piping systems are designed for WFI (Water for Injection), clean steam, and pure gas distribution in GMP-certified production facilities.

Ultra-Pure SS 316L Electropolished Tubes WFI-Grade Piping GMP Certified Zero Contamination
