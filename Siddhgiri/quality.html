Our Commitment

Quality is Not a Step — It's Our Culture

At Siddhgiri Overseas, every product passes through a rigorous multi-stage quality control process before it leaves our facility. From raw material inspection to final dispatch, we ensure every component meets the exact specifications demanded by global standards.

How We Do It

Our Quality Control Process

01

Raw Material Inspection

Every incoming batch is inspected for chemical composition, mechanical properties, and surface defects before entering production.

02

Chemical Analysis

Spectrometric analysis verifies that material grades match ASTM, ASME, and DIN specifications precisely.

03

Mechanical Testing

Tensile strength, yield strength, elongation, hardness, and impact tests are conducted as per applicable standards.

04

Non-Destructive Testing

Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Radiography (RT), Magnetic Particle (MT) and Dye Penetrant (PT) tests detect hidden defects.

05

Dimensional Inspection

Precise dimensional checks using calibrated gauges ensure every product matches the required specifications and tolerances.

06

Third-Party Inspection

Independent inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas, TUV, and Lloyds ensures unbiased verification of quality and compliance.

07

Mill Test Certificates

EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2 Mill Test Certificates are provided for every shipment, fully traceable to the heat number.

08

Packing & Dispatch

Products are packed in wooden crates, bundles, or pallets with end caps and protective covers to prevent damage in transit.

In-House Capabilities

Testing Methods We Perform

Our in-house laboratory is equipped with advanced instruments for comprehensive material and product testing.

Spectro Chemical Analysis

Optical emission spectrometry for grade verification

Tensile & Yield Strength Test

Universal Testing Machine (UTM) as per ASTM A370

Hardness Test

Brinell, Rockwell, and Vickers hardness testing

Impact / Charpy Test

Notch toughness at sub-zero and ambient temperatures

Hydrostatic Pressure Test

Water pressure testing to verify leak-free integrity

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Internal defect detection per ASTM E213 / E114

Radiographic Testing (RT)

X-ray / Gamma-ray weld inspection per ASTM E94

Dye Penetrant Test (PT)

Surface crack detection on machined and welded areas

Magnetic Particle Test (MT)

Surface and near-surface flaw detection on ferrous materials

Intergranular Corrosion Test

ASTM A262 Practice A, B, C, E for stainless steel grades

Pitting Corrosion Test

ASTM G48 ferric chloride test for duplex and super duplex

Positive Material Identification (PMI)

On-site XRF PMI testing to confirm material identity
