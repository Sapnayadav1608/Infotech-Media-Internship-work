Who We Are

Globally Trusted Piping Experts

Siddhgiri Overseas is a globally recognized manufacturer, stockist, and exporter of high-quality piping components, headquartered in Mumbai, India. Founded with a vision to deliver world-class metal solutions, we have grown into a trusted partner for industries across 40+ countries.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility and strict quality control processes ensure every product meets international standards including ASTM, ASME, and DIN — from raw material sourcing to final dispatch.

Our Journey

Milestones Over the Years

2009

Company Founded

Siddhgiri Overseas was established in Mumbai with a focus on supplying stainless steel pipes and fittings to the domestic market.

2012

First International Export

Expanded operations globally with our first export shipment to the Middle East, marking the beginning of our international journey.

2015

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification, reinforcing our commitment to world-class quality management systems.

2018

Manufacturing Expansion

Upgraded our manufacturing facility with state-of-the-art machinery and expanded our product range to include Nickel Alloys and Duplex grades.

2021

40+ Countries Milestone

Crossed the milestone of serving clients in 40+ countries across Asia, Europe, Americas, and the Middle East.

2024

1000+ Happy Clients

Reached a landmark of 1000+ satisfied clients globally, with a 95%+ repeat order rate — a testament to our quality and service.
