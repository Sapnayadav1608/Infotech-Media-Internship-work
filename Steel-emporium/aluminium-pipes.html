Innovative Aluminium Piping Solutions

Steel Emporium is a leading stockist and supplier of Aluminium Pipes and Tubes across various grades and tempers. Aluminium is highly valued for being one-third the weight of steel, yet offering high structural integrity. It naturally forms a protective oxide coating, making it highly resistant to atmospheric corrosion and rust.

Our product range includes Commercial Grades (1100, 3003, 5052) for general fabrication and Architectural Grades (6061, 6063) for structural and decorative projects. These pipes are extensively used in HVAC systems, solar mounting structures, aerospace components, and irrigation systems due to their lightweight nature and excellent recyclability.

Technical Advantages

Ultra-Lightweight

Significantly reduces the weight of industrial structures, leading to lower freight costs and easier on-site installation.

Excellent Conductivity

High thermal and electrical conductivity makes these pipes ideal for heat exchangers and bus-bar applications.

Natural Corrosion Resistance

Forms a natural protective layer that prevents corrosion even in moist and moderately corrosive environments.

Non-Magnetic & Non-Sparking

Perfect for electronic shielding and for use in flammable environments where sparking could be hazardous.
