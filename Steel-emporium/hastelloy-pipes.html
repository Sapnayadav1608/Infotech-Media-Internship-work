Advanced Hastelloy Piping Solutions

Steel Emporium is a leading stockist and manufacturer of Hastelloy Pipes and Tubes. Hastelloy is a nickel-molybdenum-chromium superalloy with an addition of tungsten, designed to have excellent corrosion resistance in a wide range of severe environments. It is widely considered the most versatile corrosion-resistant alloy available today.

These pipes are particularly resistant to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking. They are used in the most demanding applications such as chemical processing, pollution control, pulp and paper production, and waste treatment. Our range includes the legendary Hastelloy C276 and the enhanced Hastelloy C22, both available in seamless and welded forms with 100% traceability.

Technical Advantages

Universal Resistance

Outstanding resistance to a wide variety of chemical process environments, including strong oxidizers like wet chlorine and ferric chlorides.

Localized Corrosion Protection

High molybdenum and chromium content provides exceptional resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion in chloride-rich media.

Excellent Weldability

Low carbon content minimizes carbide precipitation during welding, maintaining the alloy's corrosion resistance in the "as-welded" state.

High Temp Stability

Maintains structural integrity and resistance to oxidation even under extreme thermal stress and high-pressure cycles.

Premium Hastelloy Grades
