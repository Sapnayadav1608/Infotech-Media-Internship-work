IS 4923 Carbon Steel Overview

IS 4923 is the Indian Standard specification for hollow carbon steel sections used for structural purposes. These are available in three distinct yield strength grades: YST 210, YST 310, and YST 355. Manufactured through Electric Resistance Welding (ERW) or seamless processes, these sections provide a higher strength-to-weight ratio compared to conventional structural steel. They are extensively used in high-load architectural designs, Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB), and industrial frameworks. Steel Emporium provides these carbon steel sections in Square (SHS), Rectangular (RHS), and Circular (CHS) profiles, all complying with the latest IS 4923:1997 revision.

Key Material Advantages

Structural Efficiency

Reduces the total weight of the structure by 20-30% compared to open sections.

High Load Capacity

High yield stress (up to 355 MPa) ensures reliable performance in heavy infrastructure.

Superior Weldability

Excellent fusion properties make it easy to fabricate on-site and in factories.
