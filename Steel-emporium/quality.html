Quality Control Process

1
Raw Material Inspection

All incoming materials are verified against MTC and subjected to PMI testing before acceptance.

2
Dimensional Verification

OD, wall thickness, length, and straightness checked against ASTM/ASME tolerances using calibrated instruments.

3
Non-Destructive Testing

UT, ECT, and Radiography performed as per customer and standard requirements.

4
Hydrostatic Pressure Test

Each pipe is hydrostatically tested to ensure leak-proof integrity under specified pressure conditions.

5
Surface & Visual Inspection

Internal and external surfaces inspected for defects, pitting, cracks, and finish quality before dispatch.

6
Third Party Inspection

We welcome TPI by Bureau Veritas, SGS, Lloyds, and TUV at any stage of inspection and dispatch.

Testing Capabilities

PMI Testing

Positive Material Identification to verify alloy composition on-site.

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Detects internal flaws and wall thickness variations.

Eddy Current Testing (ECT)

Surface and near-surface defect detection for seamless tubes.

Hydrostatic Test

Pressure integrity test as per ASTM A530 and customer specs.

IGC Test (ASTM A262)

Intergranular Corrosion Test for stainless steel grades.

Radiography (RT)

X-ray inspection for weld integrity in welded pipes and fittings.

Flattening & Flaring Test

Mechanical tests to verify ductility and formability of tubes.

Hardness Testing

Brinell, Rockwell, and Vickers hardness as per material standards.

Chemical Analysis

Spectrometric analysis to confirm chemical composition of each heat.
