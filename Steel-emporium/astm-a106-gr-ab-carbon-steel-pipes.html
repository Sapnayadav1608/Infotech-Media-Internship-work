ASTM A106 Grade A/B Technical Overview

ASTM A106 (ASME SA106) is the standard specification for seamless carbon steel pipe for high-temperature service. It is most commonly used in refineries and plants when gases or fluids are transported at high temperatures and pressures. At Steel Emporium, we offer Grade A and Grade B variants. Grade B is the most common choice due to its higher tensile and yield strength. ASTM A106 pipes are strictly seamless and are suitable for bending, flanging, and similar forming operations. Our inventory ensures 100% compliance with hydrostatic testing and dimensional tolerances required by the ASME B36.10M standard.

Key Material Advantages

High Heat Stability

Engineered to maintain mechanical strength at elevated temperatures.

Seamless construction allows for superior reliability in high-pressure lines.

Highly suitable for bending, flanging, and various fabrication tasks.
