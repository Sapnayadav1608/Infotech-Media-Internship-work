Product Overview

Stainless Steel 304 is the standard "18/8" stainless steel and the most versatile austenitic stainless steel used worldwide. Grade 304L is the low-carbon version (max 0.03% C) which avoids carbide precipitation during welding, ensuring superior corrosion resistance in welded parts. Grade 304H is a high-carbon version (0.04-0.10% C) specifically designed for high-temperature service above 500°C, offering excellent creep strength and durability. Steel Emporium supplies these grades in various forms like Seamless, Welded, and ERW with complete MTC (Material Test Certificate).

Frequently Asked Questions

The primary difference is carbon content. 304L has a lower carbon content (0.03% max), which prevents chromium carbide precipitation during welding, maintaining corrosion resistance in the as-welded condition.

Grade 304H is recommended for elevated temperature applications, specifically above 500°C. Its higher carbon content (0.04–0.10%) provides better creep strength and long-term durability in high-temperature service.

SS 304 pipes comply with ASTM A312 (seamless & welded pipes), ASTM A213 (tubes), ASTM A269, and international equivalents like DIN 1.4301, JIS SUS 304, and BS 304S31.

SS 304 pipes are widely used in petrochemical, chemical processing, food & beverage, pharmaceutical, water treatment, and power generation industries due to their excellent corrosion resistance and hygienic properties.
