Reliable Marine Piping Solutions

Steel Emporium is a leading stockist and manufacturer of high-quality Copper Nickel Pipes and Tubes (also known as Cupro Nickel). These alloys are specifically engineered for marine applications due to their inherent resistance to bio-fouling (marine growth) and excellent performance in seawater.

Our product range features the two most popular industrial grades: 90/10 Copper Nickel (C70600), the standard for seawater piping, and 70/30 Copper Nickel (C71500), which offers higher strength and better resistance to high-velocity water flow. These pipes are essential for shipbuilding, desalination plants, and offshore oil and gas production where reliable corrosion resistance is non-negotiable.

Technical Advantages

Natural Bio-Fouling Resistance

The alloy surfaces are naturally antimicrobial, preventing the attachment of barnacles, algae, and other marine organisms without the need for toxic coatings.

Stress Corrosion Immunity

Copper-Nickel alloys are virtually immune to chloride-induced stress corrosion cracking, making them far safer than standard stainless steels in salt environments.

Thermal Conductivity

Offers excellent heat transfer properties, which is why it is the preferred material for marine heat exchangers and steam turbine condensers.

Excellent Ductility & Weldability

Easily fabricated, bent, and welded using standard techniques, allowing for rapid installation and complex piping layouts on naval vessels.

Copper Nickel Alloy Grades
