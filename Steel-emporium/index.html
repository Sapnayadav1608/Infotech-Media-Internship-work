Quality Assurance

Advanced Testing Capabilities

Ensuring uncompromising quality and precision through our rigorous international standard testing procedures.

01

PMI Testing

Positive Material Identification verifies the exact chemical composition of alloys using advanced XRF technology.

02

Hydro Testing

Internal high-pressure water tests to verify the leak-proof integrity and structural strength of all pipes.

03

Hardness Testing

Mechanical evaluation to ensure material resistance and structural durability under heavy industrial stress.

04

Eddy Current testing

Non-destructive electromagnetic testing to detect surface flaws and internal cracks without damaging the structure.

05

Flattening & Flaring Test

Mechanical verification of tube ductility and welding strength to ensure performance under extreme pressure conditions.
