ASTM A335 Chrome-Moly Pipe Technical Overview

ASTM A335 (ASME SA335) is the standard specification for seamless ferritic alloy-steel pipe for high-temperature service. Often referred to as "Chrome-Moly" pipe due to the chemical makeup of Molybdenum (Mo) and Chromium (Cr). Chromium increases the hardness and strength at high temperatures, while Molybdenum increases the resistance to wear, creep strength, and overall toughness. Grades like P11, P22, P91, and P92 are vital for the power industry. Steel Emporium provides these high-performance pipes with mandatory hydrostatic testing and non-destructive examinations to ensure reliability in high-stress thermal environments.

Key Material Advantages

Creep Strength

Excellent resistance to deformation under high-temperature stress over time.

Hydrogen Attack Resistance

Highly resistant to high-temperature hydrogen attack (HTHA).

Pressure Integrity

Ideal for carrying high-pressure steam and hydrocarbons in refineries.
