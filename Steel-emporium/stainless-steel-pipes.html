Frequently Asked Questions

Seamless pipes are manufactured without a weld seam, offering higher pressure ratings and better uniformity. Welded pipes are made by rolling steel plates and welding the seam - they are more cost-effective for lower-pressure applications.

SS 316 / 316L is the preferred grade for marine environments due to its molybdenum content which provides superior resistance to chloride-induced pitting and crevice corrosion.

Our pipes comply with ASTM A312 (seamless & welded), ASTM A213 (tubes), ASTM A249, ASTM A269, DIN 17457, and EN 10216-5 among other international standards.

Yes, all our stainless steel pipes are supplied with complete MTC as per EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2. Third-party inspection from SGS, TUV, BV, and Lloyds is also available on request.

We stock pipes from 1/8" to 36" NB in schedules ranging from SCH 5S, 10S, 40, 80, 160 to XXS. Custom sizes and cut lengths are also available based on project requirements.
