API 5L Line Pipe Technical Overview

API 5L is the international standard specification for pipes used in transporting gas, water, and oil in the petroleum and natural gas industries. It covers seamless and welded steel line pipes. The grades are designated by their minimum yield strength (e.g., X52 means 52,000 psi yield strength). Steel Emporium offers these in two product specification levels: PSL 1 (standard quality) and PSL 2 (high-integrity with mandatory impact testing and stricter chemical limits). From X42 to X70, our line pipes are engineered to handle high-pressure environments, sour service, and offshore conditions.

Understanding PSL 1 vs PSL 2

PSL 1 (Standard)
  • General quality for common applications.
  • Chemical and mechanical properties are loose.
  • Traceability is not mandatory throughout production.
  • Impact testing is NOT required.
PSL 2 (Premium)
  • Mandatory **Charpy Impact Testing**.
  • Stricter limits on Carbon and Manganese.
  • Full traceability from melt to finished pipe.
  • Higher weldability and toughness for critical service.
