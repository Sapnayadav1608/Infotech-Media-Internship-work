Lean Duplex 2304 Overview

Duplex 2304 (UNS S32304) is a 23% chromium, 4% nickel, molybdenum-free duplex stainless steel grade. It belongs to the "Lean Duplex" family, offering corrosion resistance properties similar to 316L but with nearly double the yield strength. This allows engineers to design thinner-walled pipes, leading to significant weight and cost savings in construction and chemical processing.

Due to its balanced austenitic-ferritic microstructure, Grade 2304 provides excellent resistance to localized corrosion and stress corrosion cracking (SCC). Steel Emporium supplies 2304 pipes for food processing, waste water treatment, and structural frameworks.

Key Advantages & Features

High Yield Strength

Double the yield strength of 316L, allowing thinner walls and significant weight savings.

Price Stability

Molybdenum-free and low-nickel content ensure a lower and more stable market price.

SCC Resistance

Superior immunity to Chloride Stress Corrosion Cracking in industrial environments.

Excellent Weldability

Good weldability and ductility for easy fabrication of complex shapes and structures.

Pitting Resistance

High chromium content provides good resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion.

Eco-Friendly

Lower alloy content means reduced environmental impact during production and recycling.
