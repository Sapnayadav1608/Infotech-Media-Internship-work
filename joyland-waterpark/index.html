About Us

Virar's Most Loved Waterpark & Beach Resort

Joyland Waterpark is nestled near the beautiful Jeladi Beach in Virar West, offering a perfect escape from city life. Whether you're looking for thrilling water rides, a peaceful resort stay, or a fun family picnic — we have it all under one roof.

With over 15 years of experience, we have welcomed thousands of happy families, school groups, and corporate teams. Our mission is simple — give you the best day of your life!

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Joyland Waterpark!

What are the timings of Joyland Waterpark?

Joyland Waterpark is open daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including weekends and public holidays.

Is food available inside the waterpark?

Yes! We have a multi-cuisine restaurant and food stalls inside the park offering fresh, hygienic & delicious food for the whole family.

Are there packages available for school groups?

Yes! We offer special discounted packages for schools, colleges & corporate groups. Contact us for customized group packages.

Is the waterpark safe for kids?

Absolutely! We have dedicated kids' zones with age-appropriate rides, trained lifeguards, and strict safety measures to ensure a safe experience for children.

Is resort stay available at Joyland?

Yes! We offer comfortable and affordable resort rooms with beautiful views. You can book a stay along with waterpark entry for a complete getaway experience.

How far is Joyland Waterpark from Mumbai?

Joyland Waterpark is located in Virar West, approximately 60-70 km from Mumbai. It is easily accessible by road and train (Virar railway station).
