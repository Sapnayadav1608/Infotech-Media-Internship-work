FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our packages!

What is included in the General Entry package?

General Entry includes full day waterpark access, all water rides, changing room & locker facility. Food is not included but available at our restaurant.

Can I upgrade my package on the same day?

Yes! You can upgrade your package at the ticket counter on the same day by paying the difference amount.

Is advance booking required for group packages?

Yes, advance booking is mandatory for group, school & corporate packages. Please contact us at least 3-5 days prior to your visit.

What is the check-in & check-out time for resort rooms?

Check-in time is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in is available on request subject to room availability.

Are packages refundable or transferable?

Packages are non-refundable but can be rescheduled up to 48 hours before the visit date. Please contact us for rescheduling requests.

Do you offer any discounts on weekdays?

Yes! We offer special weekday discounts on select packages. Contact us or visit the ticket counter for current offers.
