FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Joyland Waterpark!

Can I take photos & videos inside the park?

Yes! Personal photography is allowed inside the park. However, professional photography or videography requires prior permission from management.

Are the images in the gallery recent?

Yes, our gallery is regularly updated with the latest photos from the park, resort, events & food sections.

Do you have a kids zone at the waterpark?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated kids zone with age-appropriate rides, shallow pools & safety floats for children of all ages.

What events are hosted at Joyland?

We host birthday parties, school trips, corporate outings, group picnics & seasonal events throughout the year. Contact us to book your event!

Is the resort area shown in the gallery available for stay?

Yes! All resort rooms shown in the gallery are available for booking. Check our Packages page for room details & pricing.
