FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Joyland Waterpark!

What is the best time to visit Joyland Waterpark?

The best time to visit is between October and March when the weather is pleasant. Weekdays are less crowded than weekends.

How do I get to Joyland Waterpark from Mumbai?

Take the Western Railway to Virar station, then an auto or cab to Jeladi Beach Road. By road, take the Western Express Highway towards Virar — it's about 60-70 km from Mumbai.

What should I carry for a waterpark visit?

Carry swimwear, sunscreen, a change of clothes, towel & slippers. Lockers are available inside. Outside food is not allowed.

Are there any age or height restrictions for rides?

Some thrill rides have minimum height requirements for safety. Kids below a certain height can enjoy the dedicated kids zone with age-appropriate rides.

Can I book tickets online?

Yes! You can contact us via our website or call us to pre-book tickets and packages. Advance booking is recommended for weekends & holidays.
