Welcome to Joyland

More Than Just a Waterpark — It's a Complete Resort Experience

Tucked away near the serene Jeladi Beach in Virar West, Joyland Waterpark & Resort is your ultimate escape from the city. We are not just a waterpark — we are a complete resort destination where every member of your family finds something to love.

Whether you want to splash around in our thrilling water rides, unwind in our cozy resort rooms with a view, relish authentic flavors at our restaurant, or simply enjoy a peaceful picnic by the greenery — Joyland has it all waiting for you.
