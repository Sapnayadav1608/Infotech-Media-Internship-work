FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions!

How can I book a package at Joyland?

You can book by calling us, sending a WhatsApp message, or filling out the contact form above. We'll confirm your booking within 24 hours.

Do you accept online payments?

Yes! We accept UPI, bank transfer & cash payments. Online advance payment is available for group & resort bookings.

How early should I book for weekends?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 days in advance for weekends and 5-7 days for public holidays to ensure availability.

Can I visit without prior booking?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome for general entry on weekdays. However, advance booking is recommended for weekends, holidays & group visits.

What is your cancellation policy?

Bookings can be rescheduled up to 48 hours before the visit. Cancellations made less than 48 hours prior may not be eligible for a refund.
