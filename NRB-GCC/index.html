About Us

NRB Industrial (GCC) â€” Precision Bearing Solutions for the Gulf

NRB Industrial (GCC) is the regional subsidiary of NRB Industrial Bearings Limited (NIBL), a prominent manufacturer of high-performance ball and roller bearings headquartered in Mumbai, India. Established in October 2012, NIBL has built a strong reputation as a precision engineering leader serving both domestic and international industrial markets.

Through our GCC operation, we provide direct access to NIBL's complete bearing portfolio, backed by local technical support, fast delivery, and deep industry expertise. Our products serve a wide range of sectors including oil & gas, construction, petrochemicals, power generation, desalination, marine, and general manufacturing across the Gulf region.
