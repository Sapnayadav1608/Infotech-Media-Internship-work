Needle and Roller Thrust Bearings

Needle and roller thrust bearings are designed to accommodate axial loads in one direction. They provide extremely compact, high-capacity thrust support in applications where axial space is limited but axial load capacity is critical.

Applications
Electric motors and generators
Machine tools and pumps
Gearboxes and conveyors
Agricultural machinery and compressors
Household appliances and instruments
Key Features
  • Accommodates radial and axial loads in both directions
  • Suitable for high and very high speeds
  • Low torque and low heat generation
  • Available in open, shielded, and sealed variants
  • ISO certified manufacturing
