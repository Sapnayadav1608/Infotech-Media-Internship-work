Types of Alloy Steel Pipes We Manufacture

Our product range includes seamless alloy steel pipes for high-pressure and high-temperature applications and welded alloy steel pipes for structural and large-diameter requirements.

Seamless pipes are widely used in boilers, superheaters, and heat exchangers, while welded pipes are suitable for structural and process piping systems. We also provide custom alloy steel pipes tailored to specific project requirements.

What are alloy steel pipes used for?

+

Alloy steel pipes are used in high-temperature and high-pressure applications such as power plants, refineries, boilers, and heat exchangers due to their enhanced strength and heat resistance.

What is ASTM A335 pipe?

+

ASTM A335 is a standard specification for seamless alloy steel pipes used in high-temperature service, commonly in power plants and industrial piping systems.

Which alloy steel grade is best for high-temperature applications?

+

Grades such as P91 and P92 are widely used for high-temperature applications due to their excellent creep strength and thermal stability.
