Types of Carbon Steel Flanges We Manufacture

We manufacture a full range of carbon steel flanges engineered for various industrial applications. Weld neck flanges are widely used in high-pressure systems due to their structural strength and fatigue resistance. Slip-on flanges offer ease of installation and cost efficiency, while blind flanges are used for closing pipeline ends. Socket weld and threaded flanges are ideal for small diameter piping systems.

Our product range also includes lap joint flanges, orifice flanges, spectacle blind flanges, and custom-designed flanges manufactured according to client drawings and project specifications.
