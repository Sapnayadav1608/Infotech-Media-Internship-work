Duplex Steel is a family of stainless steels with a two-phase microstructure consisting of both austenite and ferrite phases, offering twice the strength of standard austenitic stainless steels along with excellent resistance to stress corrosion cracking, pitting, and crevice corrosion. As a leading Duplex Steel Supplier & Exporter in India, CEBEX STEEL INDIA supplies premium-quality duplex steel products manufactured according to international ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, and ISO standards for highly demanding industrial applications worldwide.

Our Duplex Steel products are extensively used in oil & gas industries, chemical processing plants, desalination systems, pulp & paper industries, marine engineering, pressure vessels, heat exchangers, and water transmission piping systems because of their superior corrosion resistance, high mechanical strength, and excellent weldability.

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, we supply a complete range of Duplex Steel products including pipes, tubes, plates, flanges, buttweld fittings, round bars, and fasteners in grades 2205, 2304, and 2507. Our products are trusted by EPC contractors, engineering companies, fabrication industries, and industrial buyers across India, Africa, the Middle East, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
