Inconel is a family of austenitic nickel-chromium-based superalloys known for their exceptional resistance to oxidation, corrosion, and high-temperature environments. As a leading Inconel Supplier & Exporter in India, CEBEX STEEL INDIA supplies premium-quality Inconel products in grades 600, 601, 625, 718, X750, Incoloy 800, and Incoloy 825, manufactured according to international ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, and ISO standards for highly demanding industrial applications worldwide.

Our Inconel products are extensively used in aerospace, marine, chemical processing, power generation, nuclear, and oil & gas industries because of their superior strength at elevated temperatures, excellent oxidation resistance, and outstanding corrosion resistance in aggressive environments including seawater, acids, and high-pressure steam.

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, we supply a complete range of Inconel products including pipes, tubes, plates, flanges, buttweld fittings, round bars, and fasteners. Our products are trusted by EPC contractors, engineering companies, fabrication industries, and industrial buyers across India, Africa, the Middle East, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
