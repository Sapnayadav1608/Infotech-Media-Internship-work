Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between ASTM A179 and A192 tubes?

+

ASTM A179 tubes are used for heat exchangers, while ASTM A192 tubes are designed for high-pressure boiler applications.

Are carbon steel tubes suitable for low-temperature applications?

+

Yes, ASTM A334 Grade 1 tubes are specifically designed for low-temperature and cryogenic applications.

What is the use of SA178 tubes?

+

SA178 tubes are electric resistance welded tubes used in boilers and heat exchangers.

Do you provide alloy steel tubes like 13CrMo44?

+

Yes, we supply 13CrMo44 alloy steel tubes for high-temperature boiler applications.

What certifications do you provide?
