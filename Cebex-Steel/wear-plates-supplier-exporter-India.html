Abrasion Resistance & Industrial Performance

Wear Plates are specially designed to withstand severe abrasion, friction, impact loading, and heavy mechanical stress under aggressive industrial operating conditions. These steel plates significantly improve equipment service life while reducing downtime, replacement frequency, and maintenance costs.

Their high hardness levels combined with excellent toughness make them ideal for mining, material handling, crushing, quarrying, and industrial processing systems exposed to continuous wear and abrasive materials.

Welding & Fabrication Properties

Wear Plates provide excellent weldability and fabrication performance for industrial and structural engineering applications. These plates can be welded using standard industrial welding methods including MIG, TIG, SAW, and manual arc welding with suitable low-hydrogen welding consumables.

Proper preheating and controlled welding procedures improve weld quality and help maintain hardness and structural integrity during fabrication processes.
