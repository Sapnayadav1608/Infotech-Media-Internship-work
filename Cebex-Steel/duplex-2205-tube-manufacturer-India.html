Duplex 2205 stainless steel (UNS S32205 / S31803) features a dual-phase microstructure combining austenitic and ferritic properties. This results in higher strength than standard stainless steel and excellent resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and chloride stress corrosion cracking. Our Duplex 2205 tubes are widely used in oil & gas, chemical processing, marine, desalination, and power generation industries.

Manufactured using advanced technology, our tubes ensure precise dimensional accuracy, high-quality surface finish, and full compliance with ASTM, ASME, EN, and DIN standards.
