Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between seamless and welded Stainless Steel Pipes?

+

Seamless Stainless Steel Pipes are manufactured without any weld joint, making them ideal for high-pressure, high-temperature, and critical applications. Welded Stainless Steel Pipes are produced by rolling and welding steel plates or coils and are commonly used in low to medium pressure applications where cost efficiency is important.

Which grade of Stainless Steel Pipes is best for corrosive environments?

+

Grades such as SS 316, SS 316L, and SS 904L are recommended for corrosive environments due to their superior resistance to chemicals, chlorides, and marine conditions.

What surface finishes are available for Stainless Steel Pipes?

+

Stainless Steel Pipes are available in annealed, pickled, polished, bright annealed, and customized surface finishes as per project requirements.
