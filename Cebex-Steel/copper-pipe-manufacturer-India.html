Applications of Copper Pipes

Copper pipes are extensively used across multiple industries due to their durability and corrosion resistance. Key applications include plumbing systems, HVAC and refrigeration systems, medical gas pipelines, fire sprinkler systems, and industrial fluid transport.

Their excellent heat transfer capability makes them ideal for air conditioning, heat exchangers, and cooling systems, while their antimicrobial properties make them suitable for potable water systems.
