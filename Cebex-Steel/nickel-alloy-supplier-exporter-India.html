Nickel Alloy is a group of high-performance alloys with nickel as the primary element, offering exceptional resistance to corrosion, oxidation, and high-temperature environments. As a leading Nickel Alloy Supplier & Exporter in India, CEBEX STEEL INDIA supplies premium-quality nickel alloy products manufactured according to international ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, and ISO standards for highly demanding industrial applications worldwide.

Our Nickel Alloy products are extensively used in aerospace, chemical processing, oil & gas, marine engineering, power generation, and electronic industries because of their superior corrosion resistance, high mechanical strength at elevated temperatures, and excellent weldability.

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, we supply a complete range of Nickel Alloy products including pipes, tubes, plates, flanges, buttweld fittings, and round bars in grades Nickel 200, Nickel 201, Alloy 600, Alloy 601, Alloy 625, and Alloy 718.
