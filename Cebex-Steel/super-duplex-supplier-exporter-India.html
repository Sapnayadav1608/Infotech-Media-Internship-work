Super Duplex Stainless Steel is a high-performance alloy offering superior corrosion resistance, exceptional strength, and excellent weldability compared to standard duplex grades. As a leading Super Duplex Steel Supplier & Exporter in India, CEBEX STEEL INDIA supplies premium-quality Super Duplex products in grades 2507 (UNS S32750), Zeron 100 (UNS S32760), and Ferralium 255 (UNS S32550) manufactured to international ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, and ISO standards.

Our Super Duplex Steel products are widely used in oil & gas, chemical processing, desalination, offshore, pulp & paper, and phosphoric acid production industries due to their outstanding resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking in highly aggressive environments.

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, we supply a complete range of Super Duplex products including pipes, tubes, plates, flanges, buttweld fittings, round bars, and fasteners. Our products are trusted by EPC contractors, engineering companies, and industrial buyers across India, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
