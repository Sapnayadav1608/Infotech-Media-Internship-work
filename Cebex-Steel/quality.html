Committed to 100% Quality

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, quality is the cornerstone of our manufacturing philosophy. We understand that in critical industries like Oil, Gas, and Power Generation, the performance of every component is vital for safety and efficiency. Our quality management system ensures that every product—whether it's a pipe, fitting, or flange—is manufactured to the highest international standards.

  • Sourcing raw materials from approved & reputed mills.
  • Strict adherence to ASTM, ASME, DIN, and EN standards.
  • Zero-defect manufacturing through continuous monitoring.
  • Complete traceability of heat numbers and batches.

In-house Testing Facilities

To ensure that our products can withstand the most extreme industrial conditions, our in-house laboratory conducts rigorous testing. Every batch undergoes a series of inspections before shipment to ensure it meets client specifications.

Chemical Analysis

Spectrographic analysis to verify the precise grade and chemical composition.

Mechanical Testing

Tensile, Hardness, Yield strength, and Elongation testing.

NDT Testing

PMI (Positive Material Identification), Ultrasonic, and Radiography examination.

Hydrostatic Testing

Pressure testing to ensure leak-proof performance under high stress.

Certified Excellence

Our commitment to global quality management is validated by recognized international certifications. We are proud to maintain high standards of compliance for global projects.

ISO 9001:2015

Standardized quality management system for manufacturing and supply.

PED 2014/68/EU

Compliance with the Pressure Equipment Directive for high-stress applications.

NACE MR0175

Material standards for resistance to sulfide stress cracking in sour oilfield environments.

Inspection & Documentation

Transparency is a key part of our service. We provide comprehensive documentation with every order to ensure that our clients have full confidence in the material they receive.

  • Mill Test Certificates: According to EN 10204 3.1/3.2.
  • TPI: Third-party inspection by agencies like TUV, LLOYDS, BV, DNV, and EIL.
  • Visual & Dimensional: Rigorous checks for surface finish and precise measurements.
  • Packing: Seaworthy export-grade packaging with proper marking.
