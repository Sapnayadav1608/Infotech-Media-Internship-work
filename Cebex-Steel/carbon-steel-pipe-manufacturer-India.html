Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between ASTM A106 and ASTM A53 pipes?

+

ASTM A106 pipes are designed for high-temperature and high-pressure applications, commonly used in refineries and power plants. ASTM A53 pipes are used for general structural and low-pressure applications such as water and gas lines.

What are API 5L pipes used for?

+

API 5L pipes are primarily used in oil and gas pipelines for transporting petroleum, natural gas, and other fluids over long distances.

Which carbon steel pipe grade is best for high-pressure applications?

+

ASTM A106 Grade B and API 5L grades such as X52 and X60 are widely used for high-pressure and high-temperature applications due to their superior strength and performance.

Do you supply galvanized carbon steel pipes?

+

Yes, we offer galvanized carbon steel pipes for enhanced corrosion resistance, especially in water supply and outdoor applications.

What testing is performed on carbon steel pipes?

+

We perform hydrostatic testing, ultrasonic testing, radiography, eddy current testing, and mechanical testing to ensure quality and compliance.

Can you supply custom carbon steel pipes?

+

Yes, we manufacture pipes as per client drawings and specifications, including custom sizes, wall thicknesses, and coatings.

What industries commonly use carbon steel pipes?

+

Carbon steel pipes are widely used in oil & gas, construction, power plants, petrochemical industries, and water transmission systems.
