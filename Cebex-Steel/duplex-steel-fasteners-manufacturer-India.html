Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between Duplex and Super Duplex Fasteners?

+

Duplex Steel Fasteners offer high strength and excellent corrosion resistance, while Super Duplex Fasteners provide even higher pitting resistance, chloride resistance, and mechanical performance for highly aggressive offshore and marine environments.

Which duplex steel fasteners are best for offshore applications?

+

Super Duplex F55 fasteners are widely preferred for offshore platforms, subsea systems, and seawater applications because of their superior resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking and seawater corrosion.

What types of Duplex Steel Fasteners do you manufacture?

+

We manufacture duplex steel bolts, nuts, stud bolts, threaded rods, washers, screws, anchor bolts, socket head fasteners, and custom-engineered fastening components according to international standards and customer drawings.

Do your Duplex Steel Fasteners come with test certificates?

+

Yes, all duplex steel fasteners are supplied with EN 10204 3.1/3.2 Mill Test Certificates, inspection reports, chemical analysis reports, and third-party inspection documentation upon request.

Can Duplex Steel Fasteners withstand corrosive environments?

+

Yes, duplex steel fasteners are specifically designed for highly corrosive environments including seawater systems, offshore platforms, chemical processing plants, desalination facilities, and marine engineering applications.
