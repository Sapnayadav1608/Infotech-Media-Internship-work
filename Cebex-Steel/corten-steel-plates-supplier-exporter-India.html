Corrosion Resistance & Industrial Performance

Corten Steel Plates provide exceptional resistance against atmospheric corrosion, moisture exposure, industrial pollution, humidity, and harsh weather conditions. The alloying elements such as copper, chromium, nickel, and phosphorus form a stable oxide layer on the steel surface, preventing further corrosion and significantly reducing maintenance costs.

Weathering steel plates are highly suitable for outdoor structural applications because they eliminate frequent painting requirements while maintaining excellent structural strength and long-term reliability. Their natural rust-like finish also makes them ideal for architectural and decorative applications requiring aesthetic appeal and environmental durability.

Welding & Fabrication Properties

Corten Steel Plates offer excellent weldability and fabrication performance for structural and industrial engineering applications. These plates can be welded using standard welding methods including MIG, TIG, SAW, and manual arc welding with suitable low-alloy welding consumables.

Proper surface preparation and controlled welding procedures help maintain corrosion resistance and structural integrity in outdoor applications. The plates also provide excellent machinability, formability, and cutting performance for customized industrial fabrication projects.
