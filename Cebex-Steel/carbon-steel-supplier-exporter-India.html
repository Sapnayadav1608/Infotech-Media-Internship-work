What is the temperature range for Carbon Steel?

+

Carbon Steel (CS) operates in the range of -29°C to 427°C. Low Temperature Carbon Steel (LTCS) can operate from -45.5°C to 427°C, making it suitable for cryogenic and low-temperature applications.

What is the difference between Low, Medium, and High Carbon Steel?

+

Low Carbon Steel (<0.25% C) offers high ductility and weldability. Medium Carbon Steel (0.25–0.60% C) provides a balance of strength and ductility. High Carbon Steel (0.60–1.25% C) offers high hardness and strength but lower ductility, used in cutting tools and springs.
