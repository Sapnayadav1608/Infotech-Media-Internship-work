Hastelloy is a family of nickel-molybdenum-chromium superalloys renowned for their exceptional resistance to corrosion, oxidation, and high-temperature environments. As a leading Hastelloy Supplier & Exporter in India, CEBEX STEEL INDIA supplies premium-quality Hastelloy products in grades C276, C22, B2, B3, X, C4, and G30, manufactured according to international ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, and ISO standards for highly demanding industrial applications worldwide.

Our Hastelloy products are extensively used in chemical processing, nuclear, industrial equipment, and expansion bellow applications due to their superior resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking in aggressive acid and chloride environments.

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, we supply a complete range of Hastelloy products including pipes, tubes, plates, flanges, buttweld fittings, round bars, and fasteners. Our products are trusted by EPC contractors, engineering companies, fabrication industries, and industrial buyers across India, Africa, the Middle East, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
