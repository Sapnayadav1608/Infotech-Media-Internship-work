Alloy Steel is a category of steel made by combining iron with one or more alloying elements such as chromium, molybdenum, nickel, vanadium, and manganese to enhance mechanical properties, high-temperature performance, and corrosion resistance. As a leading Alloy Steel Supplier & Exporter in India, CEBEX STEEL INDIA supplies premium-quality Alloy Steel products in grades ASTM A335 P1, P5, P9, P11, P12, P22, P91 manufactured to international ASTM, ASME, DIN, EN, and ISO standards.

Our Alloy Steel products are extensively used in boilers, pressure vessels, heat exchangers, refineries, power plants, and high-temperature piping systems due to their superior creep resistance, oxidation resistance, and long-term durability at elevated temperatures.

At CEBEX STEEL INDIA, we supply a complete range of Alloy Steel products including pipes, tubes, plates, round bars, flanges, and fittings. Our products are trusted by EPC contractors, engineering companies, and industrial buyers across India, Africa, the Middle East, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
