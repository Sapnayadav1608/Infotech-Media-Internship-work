Corrosion Resistance & Industrial Performance

Copper Nickel Fasteners provide exceptional resistance to seawater corrosion, chloride attack, marine biofouling, and aggressive offshore environments. 90/10 Copper Nickel fasteners offer excellent corrosion resistance and good fabrication characteristics, making them suitable for seawater systems, desalination plants, and marine piping applications.

70/30 Copper Nickel fasteners provide higher strength and superior seawater erosion resistance, making them ideal for offshore engineering, condenser systems, heat exchangers, and high-velocity seawater applications.

Copper Nickel Fasteners also offer excellent thermal conductivity, resistance to stress corrosion cracking, and dependable long-term performance in harsh marine operating environments.
