ABOUT US

Leading Steel Supplier in India — Serving Since 2019

CEBEX STEEL INDIA is a Mumbai-based ISO 9001:2015 certified company specializing in the supply, stockholding, export, and import of premium quality steel products. Since our establishment in 2019, we have rapidly grown to become a trusted name in the steel industry, serving over 10,000+ satisfied clients across 35+ countries.

We cater to diverse industries including Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Power Generation, Chemical Processing, Shipbuilding, Construction, and Infrastructure Development. Our extensive product range includes Stainless Steel Flanges, Pipes & Tubes, Buttweld Fittings, Forged Fittings, Fasteners, Bars & Rods, and more.
