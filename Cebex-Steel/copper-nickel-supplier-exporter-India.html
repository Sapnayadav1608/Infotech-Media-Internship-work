What is the difference between Cu-Ni 90/10 and 70/30?

+

Cu-Ni 90/10 (C70600) contains 10% nickel and is widely used for general seawater applications. Cu-Ni 70/30 (C71500) has higher nickel content, offering superior strength and corrosion resistance for more aggressive marine and offshore environments.

What is the density of Copper Nickel?

+

Copper Nickel has a density of 8.94 g/cm³ (0.323 lb/in³). The specific gravity is 8.94 for both 90/10 and 70/30 grades.
