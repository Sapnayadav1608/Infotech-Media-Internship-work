How We Maintain Quality

Our Quality Process

01
Raw Material Inspection

All incoming raw materials are tested for chemical composition and mechanical properties before entering the production line.

02
In-Process Quality Control

Dimensional checks, surface finish inspection, and process parameter monitoring at every stage of manufacturing.

03
Final Inspection & Testing

100% finished product inspection including hardness test, tensile test, and visual inspection before dispatch.

04
Third Party Inspection

Optional TPI by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or any client-nominated inspection agency before shipment.

05
Documentation & Dispatch

Complete set of quality documents including MTR, packing list, invoice, and certificate of origin dispatched with every order.
