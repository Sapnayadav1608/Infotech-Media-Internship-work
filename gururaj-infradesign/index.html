Our Product Portfolio

SS Punched Grids

Gururaj Infradesigns offers SS Punched Grids in SS 304 & SS 316 grades for resistance boxes, dynamic braking resistors, crane resistor banks, and industrial motor control applications with reliable heat dissipation.

  • Material: SS 304 / SS 316
  • Current Rating: 10 Amps to 800 Amps
  • Duty Factors: 25%, 40%, 60%, 100% ED
  • Cooling Type: Air Cooled
  • Used in EOT Cranes, Hoists & Mills
Domestic Export

SS Blanks

Gururaj Infradesigns offers Industrial Steel Blanks in SS 304, SS 316, Mild Steel & Galvanized Steel for fabrication, engineering, electrical panels, HVAC, and architectural applications with excellent dimensional accuracy.

  • Grades: SS 304, SS 316, MS, GI
  • Thickness: 0.5mm to 25mm
  • Finish: 2B, Mirror, Matte, No.4, HDG
  • Shapes: Circle, Square, Rectangle, Custom
  • Cutting: Punched, Laser Cut, Plasma Cut
Domestic Export

Designer & PVD Sheets

Gururaj Infradesigns offers SS Designer & PVD Sheets in SS 304 & SS 316 with Rose Gold, Gold, Bronze, Mirror, Hairline, Embossed, and Etched finishes for elevator cabins, luxury interiors & wall cladding.

  • PVD Colors: Gold, Rose Gold, Bronze, Black
  • Finishes: Mirror, Hairline, Etched, Embossed
  • Thickness: 0.5mm to 3mm
  • Scratch & Corrosion Resistant Coating
  • Elevator Cabins & Luxury Interiors
Elevator Specialists

SS Railing Systems

Gururaj Infradesigns offers SS Railing Systems in SS 304 & SS 316 grades with mirror, satin, matte, and hairline finishes for staircases, balconies, glass railings, commercial buildings & architectural applications.

  • Grades: SS 304 & SS 316 (Marine Grade)
  • Pipe Dia: 25mm to 63mm
  • Finish: Mirror, Satin, Matte, Hairline
  • Glass, Pipe & Rod Infill Options
  • Floor Mounted & Side Mounted
Domestic Export

SS Tactile Indicators

Gururaj Infradesigns offers SS Tactile Indicators in SS 304 & SS 316 with dome and directional bar patterns compliant with IS 15477 & ADA standards for metro stations, airports, hospitals & public spaces.

  • Grades: SS 304, SS 316
  • Pattern: Dome & Directional Bar
  • Compliant with IS 15477 & ADA
  • Anti-Skid Surface & Weather Resistant
  • Stud Type & Strip Type Available
Domestic Export

Industrial Fasteners

Gururaj Infradesigns supplies SS, Nickel Alloy, Alloy Steel & Carbon Steel Fasteners manufactured to international standards for construction, marine, chemical, petrochemical & industrial applications.

  • Grades: SS 201, 202, 304, 316, 316L, 904L
  • Hex Bolts, Stud Bolts, Nuts & Washers
  • Standards: ASTM, DIN, ISO, BS
  • Size Range: M3 to M100
  • Nickel Alloy & Alloy Steel Available

SS Pipe Fittings

Gururaj Infradesigns supplies SS, Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel & Nickel Alloy Pipe Fittings for oil & gas, chemical, pharmaceutical, food processing & industrial piping applications.

  • Grades: SS 304, 316, 316L, 321, 347
  • Elbows, Tees, Reducers, Caps & Couplings
  • Standards: ASTM A403, ASME B16.9, DIN
  • Seamless & Welded Construction
  • Size Range: ½" to 48" NB
