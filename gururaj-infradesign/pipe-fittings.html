FAQs

Pipe fittings are components used to connect, redirect, control, terminate, or change the diameter of piping systems. They play a crucial role in industries such as oil and gas, chemical processing, power generation, pharmaceuticals, and water treatment.

Pipe fittings are manufactured using various materials depending on the application requirements. The most common options include Stainless Steel Pipe Fittings, Alloy Steel Pipe Fittings, Carbon Steel Pipe Fittings, and Nickel Alloy Pipe Fittings, each offering unique performance characteristics.

Common pipe fitting types include elbows, tees, reducers, pipe caps, stub ends, crosses, laterals, and return bends. These fittings are available in seamless, welded, and fabricated constructions to suit different industrial requirements.

Stainless Steel Pipe Fittings provide excellent corrosion resistance and hygienic properties, making them suitable for chemical and food industries. Carbon Steel Pipe Fittings offer high strength and cost efficiency, making them widely used in structural, oil, gas, and general industrial applications.
