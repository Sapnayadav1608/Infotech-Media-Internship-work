What are Stainless Steel Tactile Indicators?

Stainless Steel Tactile Indicators are specialized accessibility products designed to provide tactile guidance and warning signals for visually impaired individuals in public and commercial spaces. These tactile indicators are installed on floors, walkways, staircases, ramps, railway platforms, and pedestrian pathways to improve navigation safety and accessibility.

The raised tactile patterns help pedestrians identify directional pathways, hazard zones, and safe movement areas through foot contact or cane detection. Stainless steel tactile indicators are widely preferred due to their excellent corrosion resistance, anti-skid surface, high durability, and modern architectural appearance.
