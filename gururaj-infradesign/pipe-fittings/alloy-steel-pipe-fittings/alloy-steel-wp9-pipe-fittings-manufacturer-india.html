Understanding ASTM A234 WP9 Pipe Fittings

ASTM A234 WP9 is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel grade containing approximately 8–10% chromium and 0.90–1.10% molybdenum. This composition provides superior resistance to oxidation, scaling, and thermal fatigue, making it ideal for demanding environments where standard carbon steel fittings may not perform effectively.

As part of our extensive range of Alloy Steel Pipe Fittings, ASTM A234 WP9 fittings are widely used in steam pipelines, superheater systems, heat exchangers, and refinery processing units where reliability at elevated temperatures is essential.
