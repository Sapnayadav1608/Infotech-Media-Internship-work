Understanding ASTM A234 WP11 Pipe Fittings

ASTM A234 WP11 is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel grade containing approximately 1.00–1.50% chromium and 0.44–0.65% molybdenum. The chromium content improves oxidation resistance, while molybdenum enhances creep strength and high-temperature stability. These characteristics make WP11 Pipe Fittings suitable for steam services, boiler systems, and process piping exposed to elevated temperatures.

As part of our extensive range of Alloy Steel Pipe Fittings, ASTM A234 WP11 fittings are widely selected for applications requiring dependable performance, cost efficiency, and moderate high-temperature resistance.
