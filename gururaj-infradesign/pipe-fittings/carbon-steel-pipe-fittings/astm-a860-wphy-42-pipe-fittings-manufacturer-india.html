About ASTM A860 WPHY 42 Pipe Fittings

ASTM A860 WPHY 42 is a high-yield carbon steel material specification covering wrought buttweld fittings intended for high-pressure transmission and distribution systems. Compared to standard carbon steel grades, WPHY 42 offers higher yield strength and improved mechanical properties, making it suitable for applications involving demanding operating conditions. As part of the broader range of Carbon Steel Pipe Fittings, ASTM A860 WPHY 42 is commonly selected for pipeline and energy-sector projects.

ASTM A860 WPHY 42 Pipe Fittings are designed to handle higher stresses while maintaining excellent weldability and fabrication characteristics. Their combination of strength, toughness, and reliability makes them a preferred choice for critical piping systems across various industries.
