Who We Are

A Legacy of Precision & Trust

Gururaj Infradesigns Pvt. Ltd. is a Mumbai-based, ISO certified manufacturer and global exporter of premium stainless steel products, serving industries across India and 20+ countries worldwide.

Founded with a vision to deliver world-class stainless steel solutions, we have grown into a trusted name in the industry. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is equipped with modern CNC machinery, precision cutting tools, and quality testing equipment — ensuring every product meets the highest standards.

From architectural applications to heavy industrial use, our product range covers SS Punched Grids, Blanks, Designer & PVD Sheets, Railing Systems, Tactile Indicators, Fasteners, and Pipe Fittings — all crafted with precision and care.

Our Story

Our Journey

Year 1
Foundation & Beginnings

Gururaj Infradesigns was established in Mumbai with a small team and a big vision — to manufacture world-class stainless steel products for the Indian market.

Year 3
Product Range Expansion

Expanded our product portfolio to include SS Punched Grids, Blanks, Railing Systems and Tactile Indicators — catering to architecture, infrastructure and industrial sectors.

Year 5
ISO Certification Achieved

Received ISO certification for our manufacturing processes — a milestone that validated our commitment to quality and opened doors to larger domestic contracts.

Year 7
Global Export Launch

Began exporting to international markets — starting with the Middle East and Southeast Asia. Introduced Designer & PVD Sheets and Industrial Fasteners for global demand.

Year 10
20+ Countries Milestone

Achieved the landmark of exporting to 20+ countries across Europe, USA, Middle East and Asia — with 500+ satisfied clients and a growing global reputation.

Today
Leading & Growing

Today, Gururaj Infradesigns stands as a trusted name in the SS industry — continuously innovating, expanding our product range, and delivering excellence to clients worldwide.
