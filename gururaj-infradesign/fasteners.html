FAQs

Industrial fasteners are mechanical components used to join, secure, or assemble two or more parts together. They are widely used in industries such as construction, oil and gas, power generation, automotive, marine, petrochemical, and heavy engineering.

Common types of fasteners include bolts, nuts, screws, washers, studs, threaded rods, anchor bolts, U-bolts, and socket head cap screws. The appropriate type depends on the application's load requirements, environmental conditions, and installation method.

Fasteners are manufactured using different materials based on strength and corrosion resistance requirements. The most commonly used materials include Stainless Steel Fasteners, Alloy Steel Fasteners, Carbon Steel Fasteners, and Nickel Alloy Fasteners.

Bolts are typically used with nuts to secure components together, screws create their own mating threads or engage tapped holes, while studs are threaded rods designed for applications requiring repeated assembly and disassembly.
