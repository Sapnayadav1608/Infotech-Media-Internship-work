Precision Compressed Air Distribution

Renaissance Fittings and Pipes is a trusted air header manufacturer, supplier, and exporter, delivering high-performance air headers designed for efficient compressed air distribution.

An air header is not just a fabricated pipe with outlets—it plays a critical role in how smoothly an instrumentation or pneumatic system operates. A poorly designed header can lead to pressure drops, uneven flow, or installation challenges.

Whether you require a standard configuration or a fully customized unit, we manufacture air headers that are built to perform consistently in demanding industrial environments, working closely with EPC contractors and plant engineers worldwide.

Industrial Air Header Solutions for Reliable Distribution

Designed for Practical Use in Real Systems

In most industrial setups, the goal is to connect multiple lines in a way that keeps the system manageable. Air headers simplify this by acting as a central distribution point, eliminating complex piping layouts. This approach saves time and effort during long-term maintenance.

Built for Consistent Pressure and Flow

Maintaining equal pressure across all outlets is critical. Our air headers are manufactured with proper internal flow considerations and accurate outlet placement, ensuring that each connection performs as expected, especially in sensitive instrumentation systems.

Air Header Configurations We Manufacture

Flexible Outlet Design Based on Requirement

Every project has different outlet count and spacing requirements. We manufacture air headers with flexible designs, allowing buyers to choose exactly what fits their system instead of adjusting their system to fit the product.

Threaded, Welded and Flanged Options

Connection type plays a major role in installation. We ensure compatibility with global standards such as NPT, BSP, and BSPT, offering threaded, socket weld, and flanged types based on required strength.

Integrated Valves, Drain and Vent Options

We offer ready-for-use air headers with drain valves, vent plugs, and isolation valves integrated. These additions improve system control and reduce extra installation work on-site.

Manufacturing Precision and Fitment Accuracy

Alignment and fitment matter more than theoretical specs. If outlet spacing is slightly off, installation becomes difficult. Each air header is manufactured with strict attention to dimensional accuracy to ensure a perfect on-site fit.

Materials of Construction for Air Headers

Stainless Steel and Carbon Steel Options

Manufactured in SS304, SS316, SS316L, and Carbon Steel. Stainless steel is preferred for corrosive environments, while carbon steel is used in general industrial applications.

Choosing the Right Material for Your Application

Material selection directly impacts performance and lifespan. We assist buyers in selecting the most suitable material based on actual usage conditions, moisture levels, and chemical exposure.
