Precision Tube Fittings for Reliable Piping and Instrumentation Systems

What Tube Fittings Do in High-Pressure Systems

Designed to connect tubes ensuring secure, leak-proof flow of liquids or gases. In high-pressure systems, even a minor failure can lead to operational risk. Modern systems widely use double ferrule designs, where sealing depends on uniform pressure distribution.

Why Engineering Accuracy Matters in Tube Connections

Inconsistent dimensions or poor machining can result in leakage or vibration loosening. Our fittings are manufactured with tight tolerances and controlled surface finishes, ensuring compatibility with both seamless and welded tube systems.

Types of Tube Fittings We Manufacture

Instrumentation and Compression Tube Fittings

Absolute sealing performance for precision flow control. Compression fittings, especially double ferrule designs, are preferred for high vibration environments.

Ferrule, Connector and Process Fittings

Complete range including connectors, unions, elbows, and tees. Stainless Steel Ferrule Fittings ensure easy installation in process industries.

Material-Based Tube Fittings for Industrial Use

From standard SS 304 fittings to Duplex tube fittings for offshore and marine systems requiring superior stress corrosion resistance.
