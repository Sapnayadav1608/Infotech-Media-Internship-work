Overview

Overview of Double Row Self-Aligning Ball Bearings

Double Row Self-Aligning Ball Bearings are designed with two rows of balls and a common spherical raceway in the outer ring, enabling automatic alignment correction. This unique internal construction allows these bearings to compensate for angular misalignment between the shaft and housing, improving operational stability and reducing stress on connected components.

Available in cylindrical and tapered bore configurations, Double Row Self-Aligning Ball Bearings provide installation flexibility and adaptability for varied industrial setups. Their ability to maintain smooth rotation under misalignment conditions makes them ideal for applications where mounting precision cannot be guaranteed or where shaft deflection occurs during operation.

Double Row Self-Aligning Ball Bearings are widely used in industries where alignment flexibility and consistent rotation are essential.

Agricultural Machinery

Double Row Self-Aligning Ball Bearings are ideal for agricultural equipment where uneven terrain and fluctuating loads can cause shaft misalignment. Their self-aligning capability ensures smooth operation and reduced wear in field applications.

Conveyor Systems

In material handling systems, Double Row Self-Aligning Ball Bearings help maintain stable shaft rotation even when minor alignment deviations occur during installation or continuous operation.
