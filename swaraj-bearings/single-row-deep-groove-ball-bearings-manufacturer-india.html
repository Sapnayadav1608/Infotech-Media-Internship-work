Overview

Overview of Single Row Deep Groove Ball Bearings

Single row deep groove ball bearings are characterized by their simple design, deep raceway grooves, and ability to operate at high rotational speeds. The deep groove geometry allows the bearing to accommodate both radial and axial loads efficiently while maintaining smooth and quiet performance. These bearings require minimal maintenance and are available in multiple sealing and shielding configurations to suit different operating environments.

Due to their compact design and standardized dimensions, they are easy to install and replace in various mechanical systems. Their balanced performance, reliability, and affordability make them an essential component in electric motors, pumps, compressors, gearboxes, conveyors, and general industrial machinery, where consistent operation and long service life are critical.

Variants

Types of Single Row Deep Groove Ball Bearings

To suit different operating environments, we provide the following types:

Open Type Bearings

Open bearings are suitable for applications where lubrication is provided externally and the operating environment is relatively clean. They allow higher speeds and are easy to maintain.

ZZ Shielded Bearings

ZZ bearings are equipped with metal shields on both sides to protect internal components from dust and small particles. They are pre-lubricated and ideal for moderate contamination environments.

2RS Sealed Bearings

2RS bearings feature rubber seals on both sides, providing enhanced protection against dust, moisture, and contaminants. Pre-greased and designed for longer service life with minimal maintenance.
