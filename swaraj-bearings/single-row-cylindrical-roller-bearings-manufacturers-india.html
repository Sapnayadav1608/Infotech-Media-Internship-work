Overview

Overview of Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings

Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings are designed with cylindrical rollers in linear contact with the raceways, allowing them to support significantly higher radial loads. The internal construction of Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings minimizes stress concentration and improves operational durability in demanding industrial environments.

Available in multiple design configurations, Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings provide flexibility for axial displacement and shaft positioning. These bearings are commonly used in gearboxes, electric motors, rolling mills, and heavy machinery where high radial load capacity and structural rigidity are critical.

Cylindrical Roller NU Series

The NU Cylindrical Roller Bearings feature a flangeless inner ring, allowing axial shaft displacement in both directions. This free‑displacement layout is ideal for machinery facing substantial thermal expansion during continuous operations.

Cylindrical Roller NJ Series

The NJ Cylindrical Roller Bearings use two integral flanges on the outer ring and a single fixed rib on the inner ring to provide axial location in one direction. They are widely used in gearboxes where unidirectional thrust forces must be securely contained.

Semi-Locating High Radial Load
View NJ Series
Cylindrical Roller NF Series

The NF Cylindrical Roller Bearingsfeature two ribs on the inner ring and a single rib on the outer ring, offering axial positioning from the opposite direction of NJ bearings. This configuration ensures stable shaft tracking under punishing radial loads.

Semi-Locating High Radial Load
View NF Series
Cylindrical Roller N Series

The N Cylindrical Roller Bearingsare designed with two integral flanges on the inner ring and a flangeless outer ring, permitting unrestricted axial displacement of the housing. They are commonly installed where independent outer ring mounting is required.

Free Displacement High Radial Load

Cylindrical Roller NUP Series

The NUP Cylindrical Roller Bearings use two ribs on the outer ring, a fixed inner rib, and a loose flange washer to provide precise axial location in both directions. This design is perfect for applications requiring zero lateral drift.

Types of Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings
