Overview

Overview of Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings

Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings are designed with cylindrical rollers in linear contact with the raceways, allowing them to support significantly higher radial loads. The internal construction of Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings minimizes stress concentration and improves operational durability in demanding industrial environments.

Available in multiple design configurations, Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings provide flexibility for axial displacement and shaft positioning. These bearings are commonly used in gearboxes, electric motors, rolling mills, and heavy machinery where high radial load capacity and structural rigidity are critical.

Variants

Types of Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings

To suit varied industrial requirements, we provide the following configurations:

Free-Displacement Type

NU and N series Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings allow axial displacement to accommodate thermal expansion during operation.

Semi-Locating Type

NJ and NF series Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings provide axial positioning in one direction while supporting high radial loads.

Locating Type

NUP series Single Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings are designed for axial location in both directions, ensuring precise shaft positioning.
