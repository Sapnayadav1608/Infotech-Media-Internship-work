Overview

Overview of Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings

Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings are designed with asymmetrical rollers arranged at an angle to the bearing axis, allowing them to support extremely high axial loads. The spherical raceway in the housing washer enables Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings to provide self-aligning capability, compensating for shaft misalignment and housing deflection during operation.

The self-aligning feature of Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings reduces stress concentration and improves operational stability under heavy loads. These bearings can accommodate limited radial loads in addition to high axial loads, making Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings ideal for heavy-duty vertical applications. Their rugged construction and optimized internal geometry ensure long service life even in demanding industrial conditions.
