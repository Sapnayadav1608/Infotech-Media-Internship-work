Overview

Overview of 7200 Angular Contact Ball Bearings

The 7200 Angular Contact Ball Bearings are designed with raceways that are offset relative to each other in the axial direction. This design allows them to support combined radial and axial loads efficiently while maintaining high-speed capability. Available in contact angles of 15°, 25°, and 40°, the 7200 series provides flexibility based on axial load requirements.

Because they are designed for single-direction axial load support, 7200 Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings are often mounted in pairs for applications requiring bidirectional load handling. Their precision design makes them suitable for high-performance industrial machinery.
