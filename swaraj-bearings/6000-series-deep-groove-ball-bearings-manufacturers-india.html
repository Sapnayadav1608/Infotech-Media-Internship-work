Overview

Overview of 6000 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings

The 6000 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings are designed primarily for radial load applications while also accommodating moderate axial loads in both directions. These bearings feature deep raceway grooves that enhance load distribution and allow smooth high-speed operation under stable working conditions. The internal geometry of 6000 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings ensures reduced friction, minimal vibration, and consistent rotational accuracy across various industrial setups.

Because of their compact cross-section and lightweight construction, 6000 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings are ideal for light-duty industrial systems and small machinery where space efficiency is important. Their standardized dimensions make installation and replacement simple, while their durability ensures reliable long-term performance. The versatility and cost-effectiveness of 6000 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings make them one of the most widely used bearing grades in mechanical and electrical equipment.

Available Models

Variants & Available Models – 6000 Series

The 6000 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings are available in multiple sealing and shielding configurations to meet varied industrial requirements.

Open Type (6000 Series Open)

Suitable for clean environments where external lubrication is provided. Ideal for high-speed, light-duty applications.

ZZ Type (6000 Series ZZ / Metal Shielded)

Equipped with metal shields on both sides to protect against dust and debris. These bearings are pre-lubricated and suitable for moderate contamination conditions.

2RS Type (6000 Series 2RS / Rubber Sealed)

Fitted with rubber seals on both sides to prevent moisture and contaminant entry. These bearings are pre-greased and require minimal maintenance.
