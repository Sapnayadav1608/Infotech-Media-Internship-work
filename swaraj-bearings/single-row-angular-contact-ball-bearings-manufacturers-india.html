Overview

Overview of Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings

Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings are designed with raceways that are offset relative to each other in the axial direction. This design allows Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings to support combined radial and axial loads simultaneously. The contact angle, typically ranging from 15° to 40°, determines the axial load carrying capacity of Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings.

Because Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings are designed for single-direction axial load support, they are often used in pairs for bidirectional applications. Their high-speed capability and structural rigidity make Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings suitable for precision industrial machinery where load stability and operational accuracy are essential.
