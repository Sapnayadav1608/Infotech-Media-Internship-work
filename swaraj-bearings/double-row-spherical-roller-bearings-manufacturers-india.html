Overview

Overview of Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings

Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings are designed with a common spherical outer raceway, allowing automatic alignment correction during operation. This unique construction enables these bearings to tolerate misalignment caused by shaft deflection or housing inaccuracies without compromising load distribution.

The robust roller design of Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings ensures high radial load capacity and reliable axial load handling in both directions. These bearings are commonly installed in heavy industrial machinery, mining equipment, material handling systems, and steel processing plants where durability and structural rigidity are essential.

Types of Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings

To meet varied mounting and operational requirements, we provide the following configurations:

Cylindrical Bore

Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings with cylindrical bore are suitable for standard shaft mounting and heavy industrial applications.

Tapered Bore

Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings with tapered bore allow mounting with adapter sleeves, providing better fit adjustment and simplified installation.

Heavy-Duty Self-Aligning Design

All Double Row Spherical Roller Bearings feature a spherical outer raceway that accommodates misalignment and ensures even load distribution under stress.
