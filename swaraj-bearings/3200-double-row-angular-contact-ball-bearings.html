Overview

Overview of 3200 Angular Contact Ball Bearings

The 3200 Angular Contact Ball Bearings are engineered with two rows of balls arranged in a back-to-back configuration, enabling support for axial loads in both directions along with radial loads. This internal design improves structural rigidity and enhances shaft stability under varying operating conditions.

Because of their compact yet reinforced structure, 3200 Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings are commonly used where space constraints exist but higher load capacity is required. Their balanced load distribution ensures reduced vibration, improved alignment, and dependable long-term industrial performance.
