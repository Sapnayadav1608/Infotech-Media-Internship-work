Design Characteristics

Key Features & Design Characteristics

The unique engineering of the NU design provides specific operational advantages that distinguish it from standard ball bearings or other roller configurations:

Separable Design for Easy Mounting

The outer ring with the roller and cage assembly can be mounted independently from the flangeless inner ring. This significantly simplifies installation, maintenance, and inspection cycles in tight machinery spaces.

Accommodates Axial Displacement

Because the inner ring has no flanges, the shaft is free to move axially relative to the housing. This completely eliminates internal stress and binding caused by thermal expansion during high-temperature operations.

Optimized Line Contact Geometry

Unlike ball bearings that feature point contact, cylindrical rollers feature line contact with the raceways. This distributes heavy radial forces evenly, dramatically increasing load-carrying capacity and fatigue life.

High Speed Capabilities

Ground to precise tolerance classes, the internal geometry minimizes frictional torque and heat generation, allowing these bearings to excel in high-velocity electric motors and blowers.
