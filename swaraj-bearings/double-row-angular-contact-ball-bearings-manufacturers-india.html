Overview

Overview of Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings

Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings are designed with two rows of balls arranged in a back‑to‑back configuration, allowing them to support higher combined loads compared to single row bearings. The angular contact design ensures that these bearings can efficiently manage axial forces in both directions while maintaining rigidity and alignment under load.

With predefined contact angles of 25° and 35°, Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings provide optimized load distribution and improved stiffness. These bearings are commonly used in applications where shaft rigidity and axial positioning accuracy are critical. Due to their compact construction, Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings serve as an efficient alternative to paired single row bearings.
