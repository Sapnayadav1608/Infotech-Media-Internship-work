Industries We Serve

Precision bearing solutions engineered for the most demanding industrial environments across India.

Home Industries

Cement

Cement plants demand bearings that withstand extreme dust, heat, and heavy radial loads. Our high-load bearing solutions ensure continuous operation of kilns, crushers, and conveyors with minimal downtime.

  • Kiln support bearings
  • Crusher & mill bearings
  • Conveyor system bearings

Mining

Mining operations require bearings that survive shock loads, vibration, and contamination. We supply durable spherical and cylindrical roller bearings built for extreme underground and surface mining conditions.

  • Excavator & dragline bearings
  • Conveyor & screen bearings
  • Pump & motor bearings

Pumps & Generators

Fluid machinery requires precision bearings for high-speed, low-noise performance. Our deep groove and angular contact bearings ensure reliable operation in pumps, compressors, and power generators.

  • Centrifugal pump bearings
  • Generator shaft bearings
  • Compressor bearings

Pulp & Paper

Paper mills operate in wet, corrosive environments with continuous high-speed rolls. We provide corrosion-resistant and sealed bearings that maintain reliable motion throughout the paper manufacturing process.

  • Roll & dryer bearings
  • Pulper & refiner bearings
  • Winder & reel bearings

Food & Beverages

Food processing demands hygienic, food-grade bearings that resist washdowns and contamination. Our stainless steel and sealed bearing solutions meet strict food safety standards for processing lines.

  • Food-grade stainless bearings
  • Conveyor & packaging bearings
  • Mixer & blender bearings

Steel

Steel rolling mills subject bearings to extreme temperatures, heavy loads, and scale contamination. Our heavy-duty four-row tapered and cylindrical roller bearings are engineered for continuous steel production.

  • Rolling mill bearings
  • Continuous caster bearings
  • Blast furnace bearings

Sugar

Sugar mills operate in highly corrosive, wet environments with heavy crushing loads. We supply corrosion-resistant spherical roller bearings designed for cane crushers, mills, and centrifuges.

  • Cane crusher bearings
  • Centrifuge bearings
  • Boiler feed pump bearings

Heavy Machinery

Industrial machinery demands high-performance bearings for gearboxes, motors, and drives. Our precision components ensure maximum uptime and efficiency across all types of heavy industrial equipment.

  • Gearbox bearings
  • Electric motor bearings
  • Industrial drive bearings

Automotive

Automotive applications require OEM-grade bearings with tight tolerances and long service life. We supply wheel hub, transmission, and engine bearings from globally certified manufacturers.

  • Wheel hub bearings
  • Transmission bearings
  • Engine & alternator bearings
