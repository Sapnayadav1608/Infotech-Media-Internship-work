Overview

Overview of Thrust Ball Bearings

Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings and Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings are specifically designed to support axial loads while allowing smooth rotation between components. Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings accommodate axial load in one direction only, whereas Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings can support axial loads in both directions. These bearings consist of shaft washers, housing washers, ball assemblies, and cages that ensure even load distribution and stable operation.

Available with flat and spherical seat washers, Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings and Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings provide mounting flexibility and alignment correction. Their simple construction and high axial load capacity make them suitable for industrial machinery where thrust load management is essential.
