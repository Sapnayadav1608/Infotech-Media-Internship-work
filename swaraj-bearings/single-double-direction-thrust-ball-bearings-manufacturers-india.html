Overview

Overview of Thrust Ball Bearings

Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings and Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings are specifically designed to support axial loads while allowing smooth rotation between components. Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings accommodate axial load in one direction only, whereas Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings can support axial loads in both directions. These bearings consist of shaft washers, housing washers, ball assemblies, and cages that ensure even load distribution and stable operation. Available with flat and spherical seat washers, Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings and Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings provide mounting flexibility and alignment correction. Their simple construction and high axial load capacity make them suitable for industrial machinery where thrust load management is essential.

Bearing Types

Single Direction & Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings

To suit varied axial load applications, we provide the following types:

Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings

Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings are designed to accommodate axial load in one direction only. These bearings consist of one shaft washer, one housing washer, and a ball and cage assembly. Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings are widely used in applications where axial force acts in a single direction, such as vertical pumps and machine tool spindles. Available in the premium 51000 Series Thrust Ball Bearings range, Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings provide reliable axial positioning and stable rotational accuracy. With options for flat and spherical seat washers, Single Direction Thrust Ball Bearings ensure proper alignment during installation.

Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings

Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings are engineered to support axial loads in both directions. These bearings include two housing washers, one shaft washer, and two ball and cage assemblies. Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings are suitable for applications where thrust forces may reverse during operation. Available in the heavy‑duty 52000 Series Thrust Ball Bearings and 53000 series Thrust Ball Bearings, Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings offer enhanced axial load capacity and stability. With flat and spherical seat washer options, Double Direction Thrust Ball Bearings provide alignment flexibility and consistent load distribution in demanding machinery.
