Overview

Overview of 6200 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings

The 6200 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings are designed to support higher radial loads compared to lighter series while maintaining moderate axial load capability in both directions. Their slightly wider cross-section enhances load distribution and increases operational stability under continuous-duty conditions.

Because of their versatile design and standardized dimensions, 6200 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings are widely used in mechanical systems requiring improved strength without significantly increasing overall assembly size. The internal raceway geometry of 6200 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings ensures reduced friction, consistent rotational accuracy, and reliable long-term performance in demanding environments.

Variants & Available Models – 6200 Series

The 6200 Series Deep Groove Ball Bearings are available in multiple shielding and sealing options to suit varied operating environments.

Open Type (6200 Series Open)

Suitable for applications where lubrication is externally managed and operating conditions remain clean.

ZZ Type (6200 Series ZZ / Metal Shielded)

Equipped with metal shields on both sides to protect against dust and contaminants while supporting high-speed operation.

2RS Type (6200 Series 2RS / Rubber Sealed)

Designed with rubber seals on both sides to prevent moisture and debris entry, ideal for enclosed or maintenance-sensitive applications.
