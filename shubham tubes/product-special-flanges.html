Product Overview

Special Flanges

Special Flanges are engineered for specific piping applications that require unique design features beyond standard flanges. These include flow measurement, pipe size transitions, isolation, and compliance with international DIN & EN standards. We supply a complete range of special purpose and DIN standard flanges for critical industrial piping systems.

Orifice Flange
Orifice Flange

Used in pairs with an orifice plate between them for flow measurement. Supplied with pressure tappings on the flange face.

Long Weld Neck Flange
Long Weld Neck Flange

Extended neck weld flange used as a nozzle on pressure vessels, columns and heat exchangers for high-pressure service.

Spectacle Blind Flange
Spectacle Blind Flange Fig.8

Figure-8 shaped fitting combining a blind and a spacer ring. Rotated to isolate or allow flow without removing from the pipeline.

Weldo Flange
Weldo Flange

Combined weldolet and flange in a single forged piece. Eliminates the need for a separate weldolet and flange assembly on branch connections.

Nippo Flange
Nippo Flange

Combined nipple and flange in one forged piece. Replaces a separate nipple and flange for compact branch flange connections.

Expander Flange
Expander Flange

Replaces a weld neck flange and a concentric reducer. Used to increase pipe bore size at a flange connection point.

Reducing Flange
Reducing Flange

Connects two pipes of different sizes at a flanged joint, eliminating the need for a separate reducer fitting.

AWWA Flange
AWWA Flange

American Water Works Association flanges for large diameter water transmission and distribution piping systems.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A Spectacle Blind (Fig.8) is a figure-eight shaped fitting that combines a solid blind disc and an open spacer ring. It is installed between flanges and rotated to either isolate (blind side) or allow flow (spacer side) without removing it from the pipeline.

ASME flanges use pressure class ratings (150#, 300#, 600# etc.) and are predominantly used in North America and international oil & gas projects. DIN/EN flanges use PN (Pressure Nominal) ratings (PN10, PN16, PN40 etc.) and are standard in European and German engineering projects.

Orifice Flanges are used in pairs with an orifice plate installed between them to measure the flow rate of liquids, gases or steam in a pipeline. They have pressure tapping holes on the flange face to connect differential pressure instruments.
