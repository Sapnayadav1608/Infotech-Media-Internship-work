Excellence in Metal Manufacturing Since 2011

Shubham Tubes and Engineers is a trusted manufacturer and stockist of high-performance piping components for global industries.

  • TÜV Rheinland Approved: Compliance with European Directive 2014/68/EU (PED).
  • Certified Quality: ISO 9001:2015 Management System ensures defect-free production.
  • Wide Grade Range: Expertise in TP304, 310, 316L, 321, 347 and specialized Alloy Steels.

With over 14 years of legacy, we serve the Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, and Power sectors with products that meet stringent ASTM, ASME, and API standards.
