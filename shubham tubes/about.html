Our Story

Excellence in Metal Manufacturing Since 2011

Shubham Tubes and Engineers was established in 2011 with a vision to deliver world-class piping solutions to industries across the globe. Over five decades, we have grown from a small manufacturing unit in Vasai, Maharashtra into a trusted name in the industrial piping sector.

We specialize in the manufacture and supply of Stainless Steel, Alloy Steel, and Carbon Steel Pipes, Tubes, Fittings, and Flanges — serving industries including Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Power Generation, and Process Engineering.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us Forward

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver high-quality piping components that meet the most stringent international standards, ensuring complete customer satisfaction through consistent quality, timely delivery, and competitive pricing. We are committed to maintaining zero-defect production through our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred manufacturer of industrial piping solutions globally — recognized for our technical expertise, product quality, and commitment to innovation. We aim to expand our global footprint while upholding the values of integrity, excellence, and sustainability that have defined us since 2011.
