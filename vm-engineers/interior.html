IMPORTANT

Why Interior Projects Fail — And How to Avoid It

Most interior project failures occur due to poor planning, wrong material selection, or rushed execution. What looks good initially often deteriorates within a few years when quality is compromised.

Improper surface preparation, incorrect adhesive use, and skipping waterproofing in wet areas are common causes of interior failures in residential and commercial spaces.

VM Engineers addresses these challenges by focusing on proper planning, quality materials, and controlled execution, ensuring that interior work performs as expected over the long term.
