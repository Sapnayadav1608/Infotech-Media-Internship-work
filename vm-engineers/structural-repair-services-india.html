Structural Repair Techniques We Use

Every structural repair project requires a different approach based on the type of damage, load conditions, and long-term performance requirements. VM Engineers uses advanced repair techniques that restore structural integrity and extend building life.

Jacketing of Structural Members

Jacketing is one of the most effective techniques for increasing the load-bearing capacity of existing columns, beams, and slabs. A new layer of reinforced concrete is added around the existing structural member, effectively increasing its cross-section and strength. This technique is widely used in buildings where structural loads have increased due to additional floors, change of use, or deterioration of original concrete. VM Engineers provides complete jacketing solutions with proper design, reinforcement detailing, and quality concrete application.

Fiber Wrapping (Glass & Carbon Fiber)

Fiber wrapping using carbon fiber or glass fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP/GFRP) sheets is a high-performance strengthening technique used when structural members need to be reinforced without adding significant weight or bulk. Carbon fiber sheets are bonded to the surface of columns, beams, or slabs using epoxy resin, providing exceptional tensile strength and confinement. This technique is ideal for seismic retrofitting, increasing shear capacity, and preventing further deterioration in corrosion-affected structures.

Micro Concreting

Micro concreting uses a fine-grain, high-strength concrete mix for precision repairs in areas where conventional concrete cannot be effectively placed. It is commonly used for repairing spalled concrete, restoring cover to exposed reinforcement, and filling narrow sections or congested reinforcement zones. The material provides excellent bond strength, low shrinkage, and high durability, making it suitable for structural restoration work in columns, beams, and slabs where precision and performance are critical.

Grouting

Grouting is used to fill cracks, voids, and honeycombs in concrete structures to restore structural integrity and prevent further deterioration. Depending on the crack width and structural requirement, different grout types are used — including cementitious grout, epoxy grout, and polyurethane injection. Grouting is essential for sealing active cracks, filling post-tensioning ducts, and stabilizing foundations. VM Engineers provides complete grouting solutions with proper crack mapping, material selection, and controlled injection for long-term performance.

Epoxy Mortar & Polymer Modified Mortar

Epoxy mortar and polymer modified mortars are used for repairing damaged concrete surfaces, restoring section loss, and providing a high-strength bonding layer between old and new concrete. Epoxy mortar offers excellent chemical resistance, high compressive strength, and strong adhesion to existing concrete. Polymer modified mortars improve flexibility, reduce shrinkage, and enhance durability. These materials are widely used for repairing floors, columns, beams, and any structural surface requiring precise restoration with long-term performance.

Guniting (Shotcrete)

Guniting, also known as shotcrete, involves pneumatically projecting concrete or mortar at high velocity onto a surface. This technique is used for repairing large areas of deteriorated concrete, strengthening retaining walls, stabilizing slopes, and restoring structural members where conventional formwork is not practical. The high-velocity application ensures excellent compaction and bond with the existing surface. VM Engineers provides guniting solutions for both dry and wet process applications, ensuring uniform coverage and structural performance.

MS Fabrication Works for Structural Support

Mild Steel (MS) fabrication works are essential for providing additional structural support, reinforcement brackets, steel beams, columns, and custom metalwork in repair and strengthening projects. MS fabrication is used when existing structural members need external support systems, temporary shoring during repairs, or permanent steel reinforcement to increase load-bearing capacity. VM Engineers provides complete MS fabrication services including design, fabrication, surface treatment, and installation with proper welding and bolting as per structural requirements. This service is critical for industrial structures, warehouses, and buildings requiring heavy-duty structural interventions.

Why Structural Repairs Fail — And How to Avoid It

Most structural repair failures occur because the root cause of damage is never properly identified. Applying a repair material over deteriorated concrete without addressing corrosion, moisture ingress, or load issues leads to repeated failure.

Poor surface preparation, incorrect material selection, and inadequate curing are the most common reasons why structural repairs do not last. Even high-quality materials fail when applied incorrectly.

VM Engineers addresses these challenges by starting with proper diagnosis, selecting the right repair system, and executing with controlled quality — ensuring that structural repairs deliver long-term performance.
